About Fig & Filter

Two years ago, this was a picture-framing shop.

When we took over 8 Chalk Lane, the floor was covered in forty years of sawdust and tiny brass nails. We kept the old timber counter, swept out the gilt-leaf scraps, and opened the back door to a courtyard we hadn’t even seen during the viewing.

Growing right through the cracked paving was a stubborn, slightly crooked fig tree. Naming the place took about four minutes after that. We’re not trying to reinvent hospitality—we just wanted a quiet corner to roast good coffee, bake things properly, and know most of our regulars by their Tuesday order.

Who’s actually here

No head office, no brand deck. Just the two of us most mornings.

Mara

Kitchen & Pastry

Runs the kitchen and bakes from 5:40 every morning, mostly because laminated dough doesn’t care about anyone’s sleep schedule. If it’s flaky, browned-butter, or sold out by 11am, Mara made it.

Dev

Coffee & Roaster

Roasts twice a week on the machine behind the counter. Claims he can hear first crack over a busy morning rush; we mostly just let him have that.
